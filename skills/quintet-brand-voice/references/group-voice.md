## Shared attributes
1. Personal and relationship-led: begin with family, business and personal goals, not product inventory.
2. Understated authority: explain expertise and processes with evidence and context; do not imply infallibility.
3. Long-horizon stewardship: planning, investments and legacy in a meaningful life context.
4. Boutique proximity with international perspective: only refer to specific cross-border/group capabilities when accurate for the target market.
5. Accessible and calm: practical reader-centred examples, concise sentences, no unnecessary jargon.
6. Balanced commercial voice: invite a conversation, avoid pressure and invented outcomes.

## Editing checklist
- Can the reader identify the core answer or value in the opening?
- Is confidence grounded in specific support rather than claims of 'best' or 'guaranteed'?
- Does the text preserve any necessary uncertainty and suitability caveats?
- Does it keep distinct legal identities and geographic permissions clear?
- Does it avoid luxury clichés and generic mass-market sales copy?
- Is it differentiated from group corporate copy where a local brand is speaking?

## Word choice guidance
Generally useful: understanding, goals, planning, perspective, informed decisions, family, long term, tailored (only if genuinely applicable), specialist, discuss, consider.
Use carefully: safe, secure, exclusive, independent, fiduciary, leading, sustainable, tax-efficient, guaranteed, global access; require exact context, substantiation and any approved wording.
Never invent: client outcomes, performance projections, regulatory permission, brand awards, product terms or tax rules.

## Format variants
- Wealth planning: empathetic, concrete life goals, credible next steps.
- Investment commentary: evidence, scenarios and assumptions, uncertainty, dated figures.
- Service information: concise proposition, availability conditions and process.
- Editorial thought leadership: clear viewpoint separated from fact, authored and dated.
- Corporate announcement: precise identities, dates, direct factual statements, attributable quotes.
